The harmony engine ports formulas and rules from these projects (see also `NOTICE.md`). Their
licences ask for these notices to travel with the code.

## dycon, hrep, har18, incon (MIT)

Used in `src/theory/consonance.ts`: the Sethares (1993) and Hutchinson & Knopoff (1978)
roughness formulas, the harmonic-tone defaults, the harmonicity method of Harrison & Pearce
(2018) and the combined consonance model of incon.

Used in `src/theory/lines.ts`: line rules (a leap answered by a step back, runs of leaps).
