# The electric piano's recordings

`tines.bin` is the sample table of **mda ePiano** by **Paul Kellett** (`mdaEPianoData.h`, as
published with the mda plug-ins, for example in <https://github.com/elk-audio/mda-vst3>),
written out as 16-bit little-endian samples at 32 kHz by `scripts/epiano-pack.py`, unchanged.
